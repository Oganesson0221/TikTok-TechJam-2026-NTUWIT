System workflow

Research that leaves a trail.

Every experiment starts from a bounded hypothesis, trains against the same validation contract, and records its metrics and resource use.

01

Read

Load the benchmark contract, train/validation split and target metrics.

02

Propose

Select one controlled feature, model, objective or training change.

03

Train

Run the scheduled experiment and preserve the validation-best checkpoint.

04

Evaluate

Measure NDCG@10 and Recall@50 on logged candidates.

05

Reflect

Use measured history to choose the next bounded comparison.

06

Export

Write label-blind scores and ranks with a checksum manifest.
